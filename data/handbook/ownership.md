# Metric ownership

## Who owns what
Every metric has one **business data owner**, who approves its definition and
any change to it. The data team implements and measures; it does not decide the
definition.

- Active user, activation — owned by Product
- Churn, MRR, net revenue — owned by Finance
- Qualified lead — owned by Marketing
- Checkout conversion — owned jointly by Product and Finance

## Changing a definition
A definition change requires the owner's sign-off and a note in the changelog
with the date the new definition takes effect. Historical figures are not
recalculated under a new definition unless the owner explicitly requests it.
