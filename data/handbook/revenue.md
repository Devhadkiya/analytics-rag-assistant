# Revenue

## Recognition
Revenue for an **annual plan** is recognised monthly over the term, not at the
point of payment. The cash is received upfront and held as deferred revenue;
one twelfth is recognised in each month of the term.

Monthly plans recognise revenue in the month of the billing period they cover.

## Gross and net MRR
**Gross MRR** is the sum of recurring subscription revenue at list price for
all active subscriptions at month end.

**Net MRR** is gross MRR less discounts, less payment processing fees, and less
refunds issued in the period. Net MRR is the figure used in board reporting.

Net MRR movement is reported as: new + expansion − contraction − churn.

## Payments
Transactions settle through the payment service provider, and the PSP fee is
deducted before payout. Reconciliation runs against the PSP settlement file,
not against checkout events, because an abandoned or failed checkout produces
an event without a settlement.
